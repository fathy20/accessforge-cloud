3. CRITICAL BUSINESS DECISION

====================================================



We do not want to calculate aviation hours from local assumptions.



The system must not calculate official Block Time using:



- OFF minus ON;

- startTimeUTC minus endTimeUTC;

- flight duration;

- duty duration;

- guessed REDSEA rules.



We want to consume official values from LEON and then:



- normalize them;

- group them;

- summarize them;

- display them;

- export them later.



The official duration field identified in LEON is:



blockTimeJourneyLog



This comes through LEON Report Wizard.



The system may sum official per-flight `blockTimeJourneyLog` values per crew code because this is report aggregation, not recreating the aviation duration from timestamps.
